# Marketing Studio Product Video Download Guide

## Overview

## Directory Structure
All videos should be saved to:

## How to Download Videos

### Step 1: Navigate to a source
Choose one of the URLs above for your video mode and open it in your browser.

### Step 2: Select a video
Browse the search results and select a video that matches the description. Look for:
- Videos that are at least 2-5 seconds long
- MP4 format (standard video format)
- Good visual quality
- Landscape orientation (16:9 aspect ratio preferred)

### Step 3: Download the video
Most free stock sites have a download button. Click it and ensure you're downloading as MP4.

### Step 4: Save to the correct location
Save the video to:
```
C:\Users\HAKAN58\Desktop\Wep sitem\public\assets\higgsfield\marketing-studio\product\
```

Use the exact filename from the list above (e.g., `ugc.mp4`, `gadget_saved_me.mp4`, etc.)

### Step 5: Verify
Once downloaded, the video should automatically work in your component at `/marketing-studio/product`
